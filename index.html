Different Kinds of Makeup

Here are some common makeup products and what they are used for.

Primer

Primer helps make the skin smooth and helps makeup last longer.

Foundation

Foundation helps make the skin tone look more even.

Concealer

Concealer covers dark circles, pimples, and other small areas.

Powder

Powder helps set the foundation and reduce extra oil or shine.

Blush

Blush adds a pink or peach color to the cheeks.

Bronzer

Bronzer adds warmth and a sun-kissed look to the face.

Contour

Contour creates shadows to make some facial features look more defined.

Highlighter

Highlighter adds shine to the high points of the face, such as the cheekbones.

Eyebrow Products

Eyebrow pencils, powders, and gels help fill in and shape the eyebrows.

Eyeshadow

Eyeshadow adds color to the eyelids and can make the eyes look more defined.

Eyeliner

Eyeliner is used around the eyes to make the lash line look darker.

Mascara

Mascara makes the eyelashes look darker, longer, or fuller.

Lip Liner

Lip liner outlines the lips and helps lipstick look neater.

Lipstick or Lip Gloss

These products add color, shine, or moisture to the lips.

Setting Spray

Setting spray helps the makeup stay in place and makes the layers look more blended.

Step-by-Step Makeup Routine

Follow these steps to apply makeup in the correct order.

  1. Clean your face: Wash your face to remove dirt, oil, and old makeup.
  2. Apply moisturizer: Moisturizer helps keep the skin soft and hydrated.
  3. Apply sunscreen: During the daytime, use sunscreen to protect your skin from the sun.
  4. Apply primer: Use a small amount of primer to prepare your skin for makeup.
  5. Apply color corrector (optional): Use color corrector on areas with redness or dark spots if needed.
  6. Apply foundation: Blend a thin layer of foundation over your face.
  7. Apply concealer: Put concealer under the eyes or on blemishes that need more coverage.
  8. Apply cream contour and cream blush: Blend these products before applying powder products.
  9. Apply powder: Lightly apply powder to set the foundation and concealer.
  10. Apply bronzer, blush, and highlighter: Add warmth, color, and glow to the face.
  11. Do your eyebrows: Fill in the eyebrows and brush them into place.
  12. Apply eyeshadow: Apply your chosen eyeshadow colors to your eyelids.
  13. Apply eyeliner: Add eyeliner to the lash line if you want more eye definition.
  14. Apply mascara: Brush mascara from the roots to the tips of your eyelashes.
  15. Apply lip products: Use lip liner, lipstick, lip gloss, or lip balm.
  16. Apply setting spray: Spray it evenly over your face to help your makeup last longer.

BEGINNER TIPS

Tip 1: Use small amounts of product first. You can always add more.

Tip 2: Blend your makeup well so there are no harsh lines.

Tip 3: Apply cream products before powder products.

Tip 4: Clean your makeup brushes regularly.

Tip 5: Always remove your makeup before going to sleep.
